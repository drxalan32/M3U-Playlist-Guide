TiviMate Setup Guide for UK Users

Author: IPTV Guide UK · Last updated: 2026-11-21

TiviMate is an IPTV player designed for compatible TV devices. It does not provide channels or a subscription by itself. You need a playlist or login supplied by a service you are authorised to use, and you should confirm current device support and app availability before installing anything.

1. Check device and app compatibility

Before setup, confirm your exact device model and operating-system version are supported by the current TiviMate release. Check the app's official listing or developer information for the latest availability. App features, installation routes and premium options can change, so do not rely on old screenshots or third-party download pages.

  • Use an official app store or developer-published source.
  • Review the developer, permissions, update history and privacy information.
  • Install system updates on your streaming device.

2. Prepare your playlist or login details

Ask your authorised service provider which connection method its app supports. Depending on the provider and player version, setup may use an M3U playlist URL or another supported login method. Do not guess server addresses or copy credentials from public examples.

Keep the playlist URL and account details private. Anyone with access to a working playlist URL may be able to use the associated access, depending on the provider's controls.

3. Add a playlist in TiviMate

  1. Open TiviMate on the compatible device.
  2. Choose the option to add a playlist.
  3. Select the connection type supported by your provider.
  4. Enter the details exactly as supplied, checking for spaces and typing errors.
  5. Wait for the app to process the playlist, then review the channel list.

Menu wording can differ by app version. Follow the current in-app prompts and the provider's official instructions rather than using unofficial modified applications.

4. Configure the electronic programme guide (EPG)

An EPG displays programme schedules; it is separate from the video stream. First confirm that your authorised service actually supplies guide data and ask which setup method and format it supports. Depending on the app version and service configuration, guide data may be supplied with the playlist or configured separately. Do not assume that every account includes an EPG feed.

  1. Use the EPG source or setup details provided directly by your service.
  2. Enter any supplied address or configuration exactly as provided, keeping it private if it contains account information.
  3. Refresh or update the guide using the option available in your installed app version.
  4. Check whether programme listings appear against the correct channels.

Channel matching can depend on identifiers such as tvg-id in an extended M3U playlist and corresponding channel IDs in the schedule data. If these do not match, the guide may load but show missing or incorrectly assigned listings. Read our IPTV EPG guide for a fuller explanation of XMLTV, channel mapping and time settings.

5. Troubleshoot common TiviMate setup problems

ProblemWhat to check
Playlist will not loadCheck the exact URL or credentials, connection method, device internet access and whether the provider confirms the account is active. See our M3U playlist troubleshooting guide for file and URL checks.
Channels appear without a guideConfirm that EPG data is included, the correct source is configured and channel IDs match. See the EPG troubleshooting guide.
Listings appear on the wrong channelsCheck channel mapping and compare playlist identifiers with the IDs in the schedule data.
Playback buffersTest network stability, try Ethernet where available and check whether other apps are affected. Read our IPTV buffering troubleshooting guide.
App is missing from the device storeVerify current regional and device availability with the developer. Avoid random APK sites and modified installers.

Is TiviMate a TV service?

No. A player app is software for organising and playing compatible streams. It does not itself grant access to channels, establish content rights or include a subscription. Check the source of every playlist and use only content you are authorised to access.

For playlist structure, see the M3U playlist format guide. To compare login methods, read M3U URL vs Xtream Codes. The M3U Playlist Validator can check playlist text structure locally, but it cannot test TiviMate playback or EPG feeds.

IPTV is a delivery technology, not a licence to distribute content. Use services and content only where you are legally entitled to access them. Never share private playlist URLs, passwords or activation details publicly.

Frequently asked questions

Does TiviMate provide channels?

TiviMate is a player application. It does not itself provide a channel package.

Where should I install TiviMate from?

Use the developer's official distribution information and check current device compatibility.

Can TiviMate use every playlist format?

Supported formats and features depend on the app version. Check the current app documentation.

Does a working TiviMate setup prove a provider is authorised?

No. Player compatibility and content rights are separate matters.
