DETAILED WALKTHROUGH

Complete Smarters Pro Setup Instructions

For a standard Kemo account, use Login with Xtream Codes API in Smarters Pro. Your Kemo activation details contain three values: Server URL, Username and Password. The profile name is just a label you choose on your device.

Menu wording can vary slightly by app version. If your Kemo welcome message gives you a different format, follow the format actually supplied with your account and contact Kemo support if you are unsure.

STEP 01

Install Smarters Pro

Install the Smarters Pro edition listed for your device using the publisher's official website or your device's official app store. Check the developer name before installing. Do not use modified apps or APK files from unknown download sites.

Use the app store available on your device and check the app name and publisher before installing.

STEP 02

Choose Login with Xtream Codes API

Open Smarters Pro. If the app asks you to accept its terms, review and continue. On the playlist or login screen, select Login with Xtream Codes API (some editions may label this Player API).

Do not choose a random portal, single-stream or local-media option. You are adding your Kemo account, not creating a new subscription inside the player.

STEP 03

Enter the three details from Kemo

Fill in the login form using the details supplied with your Kemo subscription. Enter them exactly as provided, including the full server address and any port number.

Any Name

Choose a label such as Kemo TV. This is only a name for your playlist on this device.

Username

Copy the username from your Kemo activation message. Check for missing or extra characters.

Password

Copy the password exactly. Check capital letters and avoid adding spaces.

Server URL

Paste the complete server address supplied by Kemo. Keep its http:// or https:// prefix and port if included.

Security: Never publish your credentials or share a screenshot showing your full login details.

STEP 04

Tap Add User and wait for the list to load

Check the details once, then select Add User. Keep the app open while it connects and loads the available categories. The first load can take longer if the account has a large channel and on-demand catalogue.

When loading finishes, open Live TV, choose a category and test a channel. You can then check the Movies and Series sections if they are included in your plan.

STEP 05

Open Live TV and start watching

From the Smarters Pro home screen, choose Live TV. Browse the channel groups, select a channel and allow playback to start. Use the app's on-screen controls to return to the channel list or switch categories.

If Live TV opens but a particular channel does not play, try another channel first. If all content fails, check the account status and contact Kemo support with the exact error message.

QUICK FIXES

Fix common Smarters Pro login and playback issues

Login Failed / Invalid Details

Re-copy the username and password, check capital letters, and confirm the complete Server URL. Make sure the account is active. If the error remains, ask Kemo to verify the credentials.

Account loads but channels are missing

Refresh or reload the playlist if that option is available. Check that the account plan includes the category you expect, then contact Kemo if the list still looks incomplete.

Channels keep buffering

Test your internet connection, pause other heavy downloads and try another channel. If only one channel buffers, report that channel name and the time of the issue to support.

TV guide / EPG is empty

Allow the app time to load the guide and use its refresh or update option if available. If the guide remains empty, ask Kemo whether EPG data is included and available for your account.

App is not available in your store

Check the official publisher page for your exact device and region. Do not install an APK from an unknown mirror or assume another device's app edition will work.

One channel will not play

Try a different channel and reopen the app. If other channels work, send support the affected channel or category name rather than your password.

When contacting support, share your device model, app version and the error text. Never send your password or full playlist URL in a public message.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Smarters Pro include Kemo channels?

No. Smarters Pro is a media player. Your playlist or subscription comes separately from your provider.

Which login option should I choose?

For the standard Kemo account details, choose Login with Xtream Codes API (sometimes labelled Player API). Enter the server URL, username and password supplied with your account. Use an M3U option only if Kemo specifically supplied an M3U playlist link instead.

Can I use Smarters Pro on every Smart TV?

No. App availability depends on the TV operating system, model and region. The publisher currently lists Samsung TV and marks LG as coming soon.

What if my device is not listed?

Ask Kemo which player and installation method it supports for your exact model. Do not assume that instructions for Android TV also apply to Fire TV, MAG, Formuler or Hisense.

Why does Smarters Pro show Login Failed?

Check the username, password and full server URL, including the http:// or https:// prefix and any port. Confirm your Kemo account is active. If the error remains, contact Kemo support and share the error message, not your password.
