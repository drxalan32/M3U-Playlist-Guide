STEP-BY-STEP WALKTHROUGHS

Device installation instructions

Open the guide for your device. Menu names and app availability may differ by model, region and software version.

Amazon Fire TV Stick & Fire TVStep-by-step instructions
  1. Connect your Fire TV Stick to your television and Wi-Fi, then open the Find or Search option from the home screen.
  2. Search for a compatible IPTV player in the Amazon Appstore. If the player you prefer is not listed, check the app developer's official instructions for supported installation methods.
  3. Install the player and open it. Avoid downloading APK files from unknown websites.
  4. Choose Xtream Codes API if you have a server URL, username and password. If you were given an M3U link, choose the playlist URL option instead.
  5. Enter the exact connection details provided with your subscription. Give the playlist a name such as Kemo IPTV if the app asks.
  6. Save or add the playlist and wait for the channel list and programme guide to load.
  7. Open Live TV, Movies or Series and test a few items. If something does not load, verify the URL and credentials with the provider.
Android TV, Google TV, phones & tabletsStep-by-step instructions
  1. Open Google Play on your device and search for a compatible IPTV player.
  2. Check the developer and app details, then install the player and open it.
  3. Select the Xtream Codes login option or M3U playlist option, depending on the details supplied to you.
  4. Enter the server address, username and password, or paste the M3U playlist URL. Do not alter the supplied details.
  5. Save the playlist and wait for the content list and EPG to load.
  6. Test playback over your normal home network. If the guide is missing, check the player's EPG settings and ask the provider to confirm the correct EPG URL.
Apple TV, iPhone & iPadStep-by-step instructions
  1. Open the Apple App Store and search for an IPTV player that supports your Apple device and playlist format.
  2. Install the app and open it. Review its privacy information and any separate subscription or in-app purchase before paying.
  3. Choose Xtream Codes or M3U, matching the connection information supplied by the provider.
  4. Enter the server URL and account credentials, or paste the M3U playlist URL into the correct field.
  5. Save the playlist, allow the guide data to load and test playback.
  6. If the app is unavailable in your region or on your device, choose another compatible player rather than installing an unofficial profile.
Samsung & LG Smart TVsStep-by-step instructions
  1. Connect your TV to the internet and open its official Apps store or content store.
  2. Search for an IPTV player that is available for your exact TV model and operating system.
  3. Install and launch the app. Some TV players may require a separate activation fee; check the app developer's terms first.
  4. Choose Xtream Codes or M3U and enter the connection details supplied by the provider.
  5. Save the playlist and check whether channels and EPG data appear.
  6. If the app uses device/MAC activation instead, read the app developer's instructions and confirm with the provider that this activation method is supported before sharing any device identifier.
Formuler devicesStep-by-step instructions
  1. Connect the Formuler box to your TV and network, then open the MyTVOnline app supplied with your model.
  2. Open the portal or playlist setup area. The exact menu depends on the MyTVOnline version.
  3. Ask the provider which connection format is intended for your device: portal URL, Xtream Codes or M3U.
  4. Enter the exact URL or credentials in the matching fields. Do not paste an M3U link into a portal field unless the provider confirms it is correct.
  5. Save the profile, restart the app if necessary and check whether the channel list loads.
MAG set-top boxesStep-by-step instructions
  1. Open Settings on the MAG box and locate the Servers or Portals menu. The wording varies by model.
  2. Choose the portal configuration screen and enter a profile name.
  3. Enter only the portal URL specifically supplied for your MAG device. A standard M3U link or Xtream login is not necessarily a MAG portal address.
  4. Save the settings and note the device MAC address only if the provider confirms that MAC-based activation is required.
  5. Contact the provider through a verified support channel to confirm activation, then restart the box and test the portal.
Hisense Smart TVsStep-by-step instructions
  1. Check your Hisense TV model and operating system (for example, VIDAA or Android TV). Player availability differs between systems.
  2. Open the TV's official app store and search for a compatible IPTV player.
  3. Install and launch the app, then select Xtream Codes or M3U according to the details you received.
  4. Enter your server URL and credentials or the M3U playlist URL, then save the playlist.
  5. If no compatible app is available, ask the provider which external streaming device or supported method is suitable for your TV.
Windows & macOSStep-by-step instructions
  1. Choose an IPTV player that supports your computer and download it from the developer's official website or a trusted app store.
  2. Install the player using the operating system's normal installation process, then launch it.
  3. Choose the Xtream Codes login option or add an M3U playlist URL.
  4. Enter the server address, username and password, or paste the playlist URL supplied by the provider.
  5. Save the playlist, allow it to load and test playback. Keep your login details private, especially on shared computers.

Quick troubleshooting

Playlist will not load

Check for typing errors, extra spaces and expired credentials. Ask the provider to confirm the server address and account status.

Channels keep buffering

Restart your router and device, test another network connection and compare playback at a different time. Wi-Fi quality and the source stream can both affect playback.

No programme guide (EPG)

Refresh the guide in your player and confirm whether a separate EPG URL is required for your playlist.

App is not available

Check the app store for your device model and region. Use only official app sources or installation guidance from the app developer.

Important information

This is an independent setup guide, not the official Kemo IPTV website. Player apps are third-party products and may charge separate fees. Kemo IPTV features, account formats and device support can change; confirm current setup details with the provider. Only access channels and programmes you are legally entitled to watch in your location.
