How to Set Up Kemo IPTV on Smarters Pro

Follow this visual walkthrough to add your Kemo subscription to a compatible Smarters Pro player on your TV, Android device or other supported screen.

Get Smarters Pro from its official website ↗Jump to setup steps ↓

Smarters Pro is a media player, not an IPTV provider. You need valid login details from your service provider.

Example player login interface. App versions and layouts can differ.

Before you begin

1. Install the player

Use the official Smarters Pro website or the official app store for your device. Check the publisher before installing.

2. Get your Kemo details

Have the server URL, username and password supplied with your subscription ready. If you received only an M3U link, use the playlist method instead.

3. Connect your TV

Connect your Android TV or compatible streaming device to the internet and open the player.

Already have your Kemo login?Go straight to the Xtream Codes steps below. Never share your username, password or full M3U link in a public screenshot.
STEP-BY-STEP · VISUAL GUIDE

Add your Kemo IPTV account

Use the Xtream Codes API method when your provider gives you a server URL, username and password.

01

Open Smarters Pro and choose the login option

Launch the player. On the playlist or login screen, select Login with Xtream Codes API (some versions show Xtream Codes or Add User first).

If your provider gave you only one M3U playlist URL, choose the playlist / file URL option instead.

02

Enter the details sent by Kemo

Complete the fields using the exact information in your welcome message or account panel:

  • Any Name: enter a label such as Kemo IPTV.
  • Username: enter the username supplied by your provider.
  • Password: enter the matching password.
  • Server URL: enter the full server address, including the port if one was supplied.

Do not use the Kemo website address unless the provider explicitly supplied it as your server URL.

03

Save the playlist and wait for it to load

Check each field, then select Add User, Add Playlist or the equivalent button in your version. Wait while the player imports the account information.

If the account loads successfully, the app may show sections such as Live TV, Movies and Series, depending on the content and features enabled by your provider.

What if Kemo sent you an M3U link?

Return to the add-playlist screen and select Load Your Playlist or File / URL or the matching M3U option. Paste the complete M3U URL exactly as supplied, then save it. Do not paste an M3U URL into the Xtream server field.

If you have both formats, use the login method recommended in the setup instructions that came with your Kemo account.

Fix common setup problems

Invalid username or password

Check capital letters, spaces and spelling. Ask the provider to confirm that the account is active.

Server URL error

Use the exact server URL and port supplied by the provider. Do not substitute the provider's public website URL.

Playlist loads but no channels appear

Refresh the playlist, check your connection and ask the provider to confirm the account and playlist format.

App looks different

Smarters app versions and device layouts change. Follow the equivalent menu labels and verify you installed the intended app from an official source.

Download and app support

Use the developer's official website to check the current app version and the download route for your device. Availability can vary by device, region and app-store approval. Do not install modified APK files from unofficial download sites.

Kemo IPTV and Smarters Pro: FAQs

Where do I find my Xtream Codes login details?

Use the server URL, username and password supplied by your IPTV provider in its welcome message or customer panel. If you cannot find them, contact the provider. Do not guess the server address or use the public website URL unless it was specifically supplied as your login server.

Why does Smarters Pro say my login is invalid?

Check that the username, password and server URL are copied exactly, including capital letters and any port number. Remove accidental spaces, check your internet connection, and ask your provider to confirm that the subscription and credentials are active.

Can I use an M3U link instead of Xtream Codes?

Yes, if your provider supplied an M3U playlist URL and the player version supports M3U import. Choose the M3U or playlist URL option and paste the complete link there. Do not enter an M3U link into the Xtream Codes server URL field.

Does Smarters Pro include channels or a subscription?

No. Smarters Pro is a media player. It does not provide channels, movies, series or IPTV subscriptions. You need login details or a playlist from a provider, and you should only access content you are authorised to watch.

Can I install Smarters Pro on every Smart TV?

Not necessarily. App availability depends on the TV operating system, model, region and current app-store listings. Check your television's official app store and the developer's current compatibility information before following the setup steps.

Are the screenshots identical to the latest app version?

They show examples of the classic IPTV Smarters interface from published setup guides. App layouts, names and menu options can change between versions and devices, so your screen may look different.

What should I do if the playlist loads but channels do not play?

Refresh or reload the playlist, restart the player and check your internet connection. If the problem continues, contact your provider to check account status, playlist availability and the specific stream. A player cannot fix an unavailable source.
