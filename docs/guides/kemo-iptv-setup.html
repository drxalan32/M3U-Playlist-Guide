DETAILED WALKTHROUGH

Complete Smarters Pro Setup Instructions

Follow these steps after confirming that your device has a supported version of Smarters Pro. The exact menu labels can vary between mobile, TV and app versions.

STEP 01

Install the correct Smarters Pro app

Open the official app store for your device and use the publisher's link to find the correct edition. On Android, the publisher lists separate mobile and Android TV apps. On Apple devices, use the App Store listing.

Check the app name and publisher before installing. Do not download modified APK files or use download links sent by unknown websites.

Open Smarters Pro publisher website ↗

STEP 02

Open the app and review the playlist options

Launch Smarters Pro and read the available playlist or connection choices. The current mobile listing screenshot shows options including Player API and an M3U URL option marked as coming soon. Do not assume every option is enabled in every app edition.

Use only the method that Kemo has supplied and that your installed version actually supports. If the matching option is missing, ask Kemo which supported player and connection format to use.

STEP 03

Enter the details from your Kemo welcome message

Use the exact account or playlist information Kemo supplied. Depending on the connection format and app edition, the fields may differ. Never guess a server address, portal URL or API type.

Account credentials

Use the username, password and server or portal information only if those exact fields are requested by the selected login method.

M3U playlist

Use the full M3U URL only in an M3U playlist field that is available in your installed app version.

Keep your login details private. Do not post screenshots containing your username, password or full playlist URL.

STEP 04

Save the playlist and check playback

Save or add the playlist using the button shown in your app. Wait for the app to finish loading. If it loads successfully, open the available content sections and test a channel or item you are authorised to access.

If the playlist does not load, re-check the details once, confirm your account is active, then contact Kemo support with the error message. Do not send passwords in a public support post.

SAMSUNG SMART TV

Install on Samsung TV

  1. Press Home on your Samsung remote and open Apps.
  2. Search for SMARTERS PRO.
  3. Before installing, verify the seller/developer is TECH SMARTERS PRIVATE LIMITED, as stated on the publisher's instructions.
  4. Install, open and continue with Steps 02–04 above.

If the app does not appear in your TV's store, check your TV model and region with the app publisher.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Smarters Pro include Kemo channels?

No. Smarters Pro is a media player. Your playlist or subscription comes separately from your provider.

Should I choose Player API or M3U?

Choose only the method that matches the details supplied by Kemo and is available in your installed app edition. If you are unsure, ask Kemo before entering credentials.

Can I use Smarters Pro on every Smart TV?

No. App availability depends on the TV operating system, model and region. The publisher currently lists Samsung TV and marks LG as coming soon.

What if my device is not listed?

Ask Kemo which player and installation method it supports for your exact model. Do not assume that instructions for Android TV also apply to Fire TV, MAG, Formuler or Hisense.

Why is my playlist not loading?

Check the details for typing errors, confirm that your account is active and verify that you selected the matching connection method. If it still fails, contact your provider with the error message.
