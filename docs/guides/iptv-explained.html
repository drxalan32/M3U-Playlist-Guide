IPTV Explained: How Internet Television Works

Independent educational guide · UK audience

IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) is television delivered over an IP network rather than exclusively through traditional cable, satellite or terrestrial broadcasting.

How IPTV works

A typical service involves a content provider, a delivery platform, a compatible app or device, and an internet connection. The exact setup depends on the provider. IPTV is a delivery method; it does not tell you whether a provider has permission to distribute a programme.

IPTV vs traditional TV

Traditional terrestrial, cable and satellite services use different distribution infrastructure. IPTV uses IP networks and may support connected televisions, streaming devices, phones and tablets where compatible.

What UK viewers should check

  • Whether the provider has the rights to offer content in the UK.
  • Which devices and apps are supported.
  • Full price, renewal schedule and cancellation terms.
  • Customer support and privacy practices.
