Best IPTV Players for Android TV in 2026: 5 Apps Compared

Independent educational guide · Updated 19 November 2026 · Android TV and Google TV

Choosing an IPTV player for Android TV is about more than finding an app that opens a playlist. The best fit depends on how you use your TV: some viewers want a traditional channel guide, others prefer simple playlist management, and some need recording or a flexible media centre.

This guide compares five established options and explains what to check before installing one. Features and availability can change, so check the current app listing and your device compatibility before making a decision.

1. TiviMate: a strong choice for a TV-style guide

TiviMate on Google Play is designed around large screens and remote-control navigation. Its published feature list includes M3U, Xtream Codes and Stalker Portal playlist support, an electronic programme guide (EPG), multiple playlists, favourites, search and parental controls. Catch-up, recording and multiview are also listed, subject to source support and app feature access.

Consider it if: you mainly watch live channels and want a familiar grid-style programme guide. Note that TiviMate says it is not optimised for phones or tablets, and some features require a paid Premium upgrade.

2. Sparkle TV: useful when DVR and timeshift matter

Sparkle TV on Google Play combines playlist playback with a programme guide and support for several source types, including M3U/M3U8 and XMLTV. Its listing also describes integrations with compatible TV tuners and media servers. Features such as timeshift, DVR recording, multiview and video-on-demand are marked as Plus features.

Consider it if: you want more than a basic playlist player and your device and source support the recording or timeshift functions you need. Check the Plus requirements before relying on those features.

3. OTT Navigator: for users who like detailed controls

OTT Navigator IPTV on Google Play describes support for M3U/M3U8 playlists and Xtream Codes, with EPG, favourites, filters, search, subtitles and multiple audio tracks. The app offers a high degree of organisation and playback configuration.

Consider it if: you like adjusting channel groups, filters and playback behaviour. Because listings and similarly named apps can change, check the developer name, supported Android TV device and privacy information before installing.

4. Televizo: a flexible option for Android devices

Televizo on Google Play lists support for multiple playlists, EPG, catch-up, Chromecast, favourites, sorting and audio/video track selection. The developer describes it as a player for phones, tablets and TVs.

Consider it if: you use more than one Android device and want similar playlist features across them. The Play Store listing notes ads and in-app purchases, so review the current version and purchase details.

5. Kodi: an open-source media centre with IPTV add-on support

Kodi's official Android download page provides Android builds and links to supported download channels. Kodi is a general media centre rather than a dedicated IPTV app. To use live TV with an M3U playlist, you can configure the PVR IPTV Simple Client add-on and supply compatible playlist and programme-guide information.

Consider it if: you want one flexible media centre for local libraries and other supported add-ons, and you are comfortable with a more involved setup. If you only want to load a playlist quickly, a dedicated player may be simpler.

How to choose the right player

  • Check your exact device: Android TV and Google TV models can differ in app availability, memory and remote-control behaviour.
  • Look at the playlist format: confirm whether your provider supplies M3U, Xtream Codes or another supported connection method. Our M3U URL vs Xtream Codes guide explains the difference.
  • Check EPG support: if you use a programme guide, confirm that the app accepts your guide data. See our IPTV EPG guide.
  • Review paid features: recording, catch-up, multi-view and multi-playlist options may depend on the app version, subscription tier or source.
  • Use official sources: prefer Google Play or the app developer's official website. Avoid modified APKs and unknown download pages.
  • Protect your login: playlist URLs and Xtream credentials can contain private access details. Do not post them in public forums, screenshots or GitHub issues.

How to install an IPTV player on Android TV

  1. Open Google Play on your Android TV or Google TV device.
  2. Search for the exact app name and verify the developer.
  3. Review permissions, privacy information, recent updates and in-app purchase details.
  4. Install the app and open it from your Apps screen.
  5. Add only a playlist or login supplied by a provider you are authorised to use.
  6. If the guide is empty, check the EPG URL, time zone and playlist data with your provider.

Common problems and what to check

  • Playlist will not load: check the URL or login details, internet connection and whether the playlist is still valid.
  • Channels appear without programme information: check that EPG data is supplied and mapped correctly.
  • Playback buffers: test the connection, try Ethernet where possible and compare another authorised stream. Our buffering troubleshooting guide covers practical checks.
  • The app is missing from Google Play: availability can vary by model and region. Check the developer's official source and do not install lookalike apps.

Frequently asked questions

Do IPTV player apps include channels?

No. A player is software for organising and playing compatible playlist or provider data. It does not automatically supply channels, films or subscriptions. You need a source you are legally entitled to access.

Can I use the same playlist in different players?

Often, yes, if each player supports the playlist format and connection method. Some providers restrict the number of simultaneous connections or supported apps, so check their terms first.

Is a paid player subscription the same as an IPTV subscription?

No. A player upgrade unlocks software features. It does not provide content access. Any content subscription is separate and should clearly explain its rights, terms and cancellation policy.

Which player is best for a beginner?

Start with the app that is available from the official store for your device and has a setup flow you understand. Televizo or TiviMate may be worth comparing, but the best choice depends on your remote, playlist format and preferred features.

Final thoughts

There is no single best IPTV player for every Android TV user. TiviMate is worth considering for a TV-first guide, Sparkle TV for DVR-style features, OTT Navigator for detailed controls, Televizo for cross-device Android use and Kodi for a configurable media centre. Compare current listings, check your device and use only authorised content sources.
