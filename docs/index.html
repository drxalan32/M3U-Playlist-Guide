Frequently asked questions

What is IPTV and how does it work?

IPTV stands for Internet Protocol Television. It delivers television or video over internet networks using compatible apps, devices and content sources.

Is IPTV legal in the UK?

IPTV technology is legal to use. The legality of a particular service depends on whether the provider has the necessary rights to distribute the channels and programmes it offers.

What is an M3U playlist?

An M3U playlist is a text file or response that can list media locations and related metadata for a compatible player.

Do IPTV apps provide channels?

Most IPTV player apps do not include channels. They provide an interface for a playlist or service supplied separately by the user.

Can I use any IPTV app on my device?

No. App availability and features vary by device, operating-system version, region and developer support.

What internet speed do I need for IPTV?

The speed you need depends on video quality, the number of simultaneous streams and network conditions. A stable connection matters as well as download speed.

Why does IPTV keep buffering?

Common causes include weak Wi-Fi, network congestion, device performance, app problems or issues with the content source.

What is the difference between M3U and Xtream Codes?

M3U usually connects through a playlist URL or file. Xtream-style login typically uses a server address, username and password. The formats are not automatically interchangeable.
