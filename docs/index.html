Before choosing an IPTV provider

  • Verify the provider's rights to distribute the content in the UK.
  • Confirm regional availability and exact device compatibility.
  • Read full pricing, renewal, refund and cancellation terms.
  • Review privacy practices and customer support options.

IPTV is a delivery technology. It does not automatically establish that a provider is authorised to distribute every channel or programme.
