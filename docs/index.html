Frequently asked questions

What is IPTV?

IPTV delivers television or video over internet networks. The technology itself does not establish whether a content source is authorised.

What is an M3U playlist?

An M3U playlist is a text file or response that can list media locations and related metadata for a compatible player.

Do IPTV player apps include channels?

Usually, a player is an interface. Any content access depends on a separate source and the rights that source holds.

Can I use any IPTV app on my device?

No. App availability and features vary by device, operating-system version, region and developer support.

How can I check a provider before subscribing?

Review its identity, content distribution rights, regional availability, full pricing, renewal and cancellation terms directly.

Make an informed decision

Before choosing an IPTV provider

  • Verify the provider's rights to distribute content in your region.
  • Confirm regional availability and device compatibility.
  • Read full pricing, renewal, refund and cancellation terms.
  • Review privacy practices and customer support options.

IPTV is a delivery technology. It does not automatically establish that a provider is authorised to distribute every channel or programme.
