About IPTV Guide UK

IPTV Guide UK is an independent educational resource for readers researching internet-delivered television in the United Kingdom.

What we publish

We publish practical explainers and checklists about IPTV terminology, compatible devices, apps, setup and common troubleshooting steps.

Editorial approach

Our guides aim to explain general concepts clearly and encourage readers to verify provider authorisation, regional availability, device compatibility, pricing and subscription terms directly with each provider.

References to Kemo TV are links to its website for readers who want to review its own published information. They are not independent verification or an endorsement of unverified service claims.

Corrections

If you notice an error or outdated information, use the contact options published on the relevant provider website only for provider-specific questions. This guide does not currently publish a separate contact form.
