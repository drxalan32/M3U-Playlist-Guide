About IPTV Guide UK

IPTV Guide UK publishes educational information for readers researching internet-delivered television in the United Kingdom.

What we publish

We publish explainers and checklists about IPTV terminology, compatible devices, apps, setup and common troubleshooting steps. Articles are intended as general information and should be checked against current manufacturer and provider documentation.

Relationship disclosure

This website promotes Kemo TV and links readers to kemoiptv.digital. Readers should treat those links as promotional references, not independent reviews. We do not claim to have independently verified Kemo TV's content rights, availability, performance, device compatibility, pricing or service terms. Check those details directly before purchasing.

Editorial approach

Our guides encourage readers to verify provider authorisation, regional availability, device compatibility, pricing, renewal and cancellation terms directly with each provider. IPTV is a delivery technology; it does not itself establish that a service is authorised to distribute particular content.

Corrections

If you notice an error or outdated information, use the public repository's issue or discussion features on GitHub. Do not post passwords, playlist URLs, activation codes or other private account information.
