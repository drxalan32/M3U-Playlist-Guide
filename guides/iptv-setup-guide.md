# How to Set Up IPTV on a Compatible Device

This guide covers the general setup process for an IPTV service that you are authorized to use. Exact steps vary by provider, app and device.

## Before you start

- Confirm that your provider is authorized to distribute the content.
- Check the provider's supported device and app list.
- Install the app from the device's official app store where possible.
- Keep your subscription details private.

## General setup

1. Confirm that your device is connected to the internet.
2. Install an app supported by your provider and device.
3. Open the app and select the provider's documented sign-in method.
4. Enter subscription details only in the official app or provider-approved interface.
5. Follow the provider's instructions to load your authorized service.
6. Test playback and contact the provider if activation fails.

## Common setup problems

**Login rejected:** Check for typing errors, expired access or account status with your provider. Never post credentials publicly.

**App unavailable:** Check device compatibility and the official app store for your region.

**No playback:** Confirm your connection, app version and provider service status.
