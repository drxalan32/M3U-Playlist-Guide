# How to Set Up IPTV in the UK on a Compatible Device

This guide explains the general setup process for an IPTV service you are authorised to use. Exact instructions vary by provider, app, device and region.

## Before you start
- Confirm that the provider is authorised to distribute the content you intend to watch in the UK.
- Check that your exact device model and app are supported.
- Use the device's official app store where possible.
- Keep subscription credentials and activation links private.

## General setup steps
1. Connect your Smart TV, streaming device, phone or tablet to the internet.
2. Install an app that your provider officially supports.
3. Open the app and choose the sign-in or activation method documented by the provider.
4. Enter account information only in the provider-approved app or interface.
5. Follow the provider's activation instructions.
6. Test playback and contact support if activation or playback fails.

## Common setup issues
**Login rejected:** Check for typing errors, account status or expiry with the provider. Do not post credentials publicly.

**App not available:** Check the device model, operating-system version and UK app-store availability.

**No playback:** Check the internet connection, app version and provider service status.

## Kemo TV setup information
For Kemo TV's current UK availability, supported apps and device instructions, consult [the official Kemo TV website](https://kemoiptv.digital/). Do not assume every device or app is supported; verify before subscribing.
